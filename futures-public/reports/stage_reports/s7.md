# s7 — drawdown-mandate simulator (real s6 daily net returns, training period)

Base mandate: target +8%, max drawdown 10%, daily loss 3%, window 60 trading days. Every day with 60 days ahead is a start (5834 attempts). Attempts overlap and are not independent; no interval is claimed.

* breach by drawdown 12.2%, by daily loss 15.6%, timeout **49.9%**
* on the unscaled path (realised 16.6%): pass 25.1%, timeout 39.7%

## Against the prior

* SPEC §9 synthetic prior: Sharpe 0.8 at 15% vol, 8% target before 10% loss inside 60 days: ~23% pass; ~86% timeouts at lower vol.
* here the programme's Sharpe is 0.06, not 0.8, so the pass rate should sit below 23% at 15% vol; the timeout share at low vol is the check on the simulator's machinery.
